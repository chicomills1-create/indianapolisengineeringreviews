How do I verify an engineer is licensed in Indiana?

Indiana registers professional engineers through the State Board of Registration for Professional Engineers, part of the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, which maintains the state's public license lookup. Before you hire, confirm the engineer in responsible charge holds an active Indiana registration in the discipline your project needs — structural, mechanical, electrical, or civil — and that the firm is authorized to offer engineering in the state. Licensure and coverage is the first of the six criteria this ranking scores.
